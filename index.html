my-expenses

個人自用的記帳工具。每個月從作者自己的 Gmail 下載銀行對帳單與收據，在作者的電腦上整理成支出摘要。

A personal expense-tracking tool. Each month it downloads the author's own bank statements and receipts from the author's Gmail and summarizes spending on the author's computer.

這個工具只有作者本人使用，不對外提供服務。
It is used only by its author and is not offered to anyone else.
